Morabaraba

How To Play:

1.Placement

Place your tokens on one of the intersections on the game board when is your turn to play. Each player has a total of 12 tokens.

2.Mills

Mills are formed when a player manages to align three of their tokens on three connected inter-sections. When a player forms a mill, that player can capture their opponet's tokens, depending on the number of mills formed. For example; if 2 mills are formed after one move, 2 of the opponenet's tokens need to be captured.

3.Movement

After all tokens are placed on the board, players can now move one token per turn on empty intersections directly next to the token.

4.Flying

If a player only has 3 tokens left, that player is no longer restricted to moving their tokens to inter-sections directly next to the tokens. They can now "fly" to any free inter-section on the board.
